# TrendPulse AI — Macro Creator Studio Edition ⚡

**TrendPulse AI** is a Generative AI-powered social media trend intelligence and opportunity discovery platform engineered specifically for **Macro Content Creators (100K to 1M+ followers)** on Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, and TikTok.

---

## 🎯 Why This App is Built Specifically for Macro Creators

Macro creators face a unique challenge:
1. **They cannot simply copy trending templates verbatim**: When a creator with 500k+ followers blindly copies a micro-trend, audience fatigue sets in and credibility drops.
2. **Timing is critical**: Participating when a trend has already reached **Saturation** yields diminishing views. Macro creators need to identify trends during the **Emerging** or early **Rising** window.
3. **High Production & Retention Demands**: Videos require a minimum of 90%+ 3-second retention, strategic cut cadence, and custom **contrarian twists**.
4. **Monetization & Brand Deals**: Trends must align with five-figure brand sponsor integration ($10K – $25K+ per placement).

---

## 🚀 Key Modules Built in the App

### 1. 📡 Trend Radar & Opportunity Horizon
- **Lifecycle Classification**: Emerging (Prime Entry), Rapidly Rising, Viral Peak, Saturating (Fatigue Risk), Declining.
- **Velocity Index & Saturation Meter**: Quantifies adoption speed vs market copycat density.
- **Macro Suitability Score (0-100)**: Evaluates whether a trend is worthy of high production value.
- **Peak Horizon Countdown**: Estimates when the trend will reach maximum saturation.
- **Niche Filtering**: Tech & AI, Luxury & Lifestyle, Biohacking & Fitness, Business & Finance, Comedy & Culture.

### 2. 🔬 Viral DNA & Reel Retention Scanner
- **Paste Any URL**: Supports Instagram Reels, TikToks, and YouTube Shorts links (with built-in 1-click sample links).
- **First 3-Seconds Hook Audit**: Grades visual pattern interrupt, auditory grab, and curiosity gap.
- **Dopamine Pacing Cadence**: Analyzes average seconds per cut and audio-visual synchronization.
- **Contrarian Pattern Interrupt**: Generates an authoritative macro twist so you stand out rather than looking late.

### 3. 💡 AI Macro Script & Hook Forge
- **4 Strategic Macro Angles**:
  - *Contrarian Pattern Interrupt* ("Why everyone is doing this wrong")
  - *Cinematic High-Production Masterclass* ($$$ visual teardown)
  - *Interactive Challenge / Turing Test* (Debate engine)
  - *Clinical Data / Biometric Verification* (Hard mathematical proof)
- **Timestamped Video Blueprint**:
  - `[0:00 - 0:03] HOOK` (Frame 1 retention lock)
  - `[0:03 - 0:12] RE-HOOK & FRAME` (Stakes and curiosity)
  - `[0:12 - 0:34] CLIMAX / VALUE DROP` (High-density delivery)
  - `[0:34 - 0:45] ALGORITHMIC COMMENT ENGINE` (Debate trigger)
- **Algorithmic Comment Multiplier**: Exact closing question + pinned comment designed to trigger 3,000+ comments to blow up algorithmic distribution.
- **Audio BPM Sync & Cover Packaging**: Text overlays and facial expression recommendations.

### 4. 💼 Brand Deal Opportunity Matrix
- **Monetization Intelligence**: Shows which brand categories (SaaS, Audio tech, Luxury, FinTech, Wearables) are sponsoring each format.
- **Estimated Deal Valuation**: Rate cards tailored for macro reach ($8,000 - $25,000+).
- **Turnkey Brand Pitch Email**: Copy-paste proposal highlighting viral retention metrics for brand managers.

### 5. ⏱️ Trend Lifecycle Monitoring & Early Alerts
- **Watchlist & Alerts**: Real-time velocity tracking and saturation warning notifications.
- **Side-by-Side Comparison Matrix**: Compare up to 3 trends head-to-head before allocating high production budget.

## 🛠️ Quick Start

### 1. Start the Development Server
```bash
npm run dev
```
Open [http://localhost:3000](http://localhost:3000) in your browser.

### 2. Build for Production
```bash
npm run build
```
